# Character Slope 2D contract — Phase 047

An enabled Character Collision 2D v2 on a Kinematic Body with an ordinary Box or Circle Collider may follow walkable surfaces in world XY. World +Y is up. New Characters enable Slope Handling with a 50° maximum by default; migrated v1 Characters retain their old motion until the author enables it. The author may set a maximum greater than 0° and at most 60°.

Walkability is per Character: an upward-facing finite top edge is walkable when its angle from horizontal is no greater than that Character's Maximum Slope Angle. A steeper edge remains solid, not ground. Scene Static Box Colliders contribute only their authored horizontal edges, transformed into world space; their sides can never be reclassified as ground by rotation. Mirroring preserves edge identity. Slope Tiles contribute a finite triangle hypotenuse. A surface exposes its ordered endpoints, unit tangent, upward unit normal, angle, source ID, and one-way status.

For a grounded Character moving horizontally with no upward intent, movement may follow a walkable support edge uphill or downhill while preserving requested world-X displacement. A finite-segment support projection prevents the extension of a ramp plane beyond its endpoint from grounding a Character. Solid geometry is swept conservatively along the proposed movement; blocked candidates fall back to the existing X/Y solver. Flat-to-ramp and adjacent support transitions are selected from eligible nearby surfaces. A downward velocity request does not prevent grounded uphill traversal; explicit upward movement does not glue the Character to the ramp.

No gravity, acceleration, jump, input, drop-through control, Dynamic Body, moving-platform carry, collision filtering, or general collision-event lifecycle is introduced. Ordinary Kinematic Bodies retain the prior Static-only solver.
