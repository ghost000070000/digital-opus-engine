# Phase 044 — Dynamic Body utility gate

UTILITY_GATE_DECISION: NOT_JUSTIFIED

This decision was recorded before any canonical Body 2D schema/runtime/Inspector promotion. Phase 044 successfully completed a qualification-only utility gate; rejecting a premature Dynamic product feature is not a blocked phase.

## Candidate model and evidence

The isolated prototype at `tools/phase-044-dynamic-prototype.js` uses Phase 043's existing Static-response movement service through a temporary Kinematic adapter. It integrates mutable runtime velocity with `delta = velocity * dt + 0.5 * gravity * dt²`, then `velocity += gravity * dt`; a blocked axis zeros only that velocity component. Gravity is a per-body world-XY vector. The model has no mass, force accumulator, impulse, friction, restitution, contact registry, Dynamic↔Dynamic or Dynamic↔Kinematic response, Tilemap physics, or character controller. The product Body component remains v1 Static/Kinematic.

The focused suite passed 15/15 checks. Free fall over ten seconds at 30/60/120/144/240 Hz matched analytic position and velocity; floor-contact partitions matched within collision tolerance. Box and Circle rested for 60 simulated seconds without sinking, bounce, jitter, or solver caps. Tangential travel survived floor contact; high-speed thin-floor, rotated Static, all four shape pairs, multi-collider, horizontal/zero gravity, Trigger post-motion ordering, and singular-parent fallback were qualified. The 20/20 isolated Edge browser cycles reproduced a falling/resting Box, Trigger Enter/Exit, Play/Stop restoration, and exact Save/Load while confirming that the product still has no Dynamic option or Gravity field.

The seeded fuzz completed 500,595 legal movement cases over 20 fixed seeds, including 100,235 thin-wall cases; 2,000 resting bodies over 240,000 ticks; 1,000 frame partitions; 1,000 parent-conversion cases; and 1,000 Body/Trigger checks. No illegal final overlap, non-finite state, nondeterministic replay, or resting-contact cap was found. The high-speed/grazing random movements did hit the Phase 043 conservative sweep cap 6,065 times: 926 Box/Box, 1,108 Box/Circle, 1,896 Circle/Box, and 2,135 Circle/Circle. The first captured Circle/rotated-Box example requested `(6.8941, -2.6634)` and safely accepted `(3.7875, -0.0000006)`, ending at approximately the `1e-6` contact gap. This is conservative truncation, not penetration; it is common enough under extreme randomized motion to be disclosed, not silently dismissed. The Phase 043 soak's historical 20 cap hits cannot be reconstructed exactly because that aggregate did not record per-hit inputs. The qualification trace demonstrates plausible cap patterns, not a fabricated identity for those 20 events.

Performance with Static geometry pre-resolved once per tick passed: medians 0.647 ms (1×1,000), 3.723 ms (10×1,000), 7.384 ms (50×500), and 4.662 ms (100 independently resting). This is below the 50 ms target and comparable to Phase 043's Kinematic service. A promoted product extension appears technically bounded: roughly one new Body type/field and migration, a small velocity state/integration path, Inspector controls/help, and expanded tests/exports; no broadphase tree or general solver was needed for the prototype. No 30-minute Dynamic soak was run, because the utility/clarity gate rejects promotion before that conditional stability expenditure.

## Creator utility and clarity

The scenarios are falling crate, falling ball, ballistic object, falling Trigger hazard, sideways gravity, zero gravity, and diagonal floor slide. They demonstrate **two materially distinct no-code motion workflows**: (1) gravity drop-and-rest, optionally with Trigger actions; and (2) ballistic/arcing motion. Box versus Circle and hazard versus debris are presentation/Trigger variants of the same drop; sideways or zero gravity merely rotates/removes the same acceleration rule. The required third distinct workflow is not evidenced. Kinematic plus authored events/timing remains a viable, explicit alternative for the other examples.

More importantly, the ordinary creator meaning of “Dynamic Body” includes interaction with other movable bodies and with the primary authored 2D world. This bounded model passes through other Dynamic Bodies and Kinematic movers and falls through Tilemap collision metadata. For a product whose Tilemaps are already a major 2D world-building capability, those are central expectation gaps, not edge cases. Visible Inspector limitation text could be honest, but cannot make the feature behave like the term implies. Calling this a Dynamic Body risks creators building dropped props that unexpectedly pass through platforms, moving surfaces, and one another. The prototype is mechanically sound in its narrow lane; it is not yet a sufficiently general or distinct no-code product utility.

## Decision

Dynamic is **not justified for canonical promotion in Phase 044**. Body remains v1 with Static and Kinematic only. No product Gravity field, Dynamic Inspector option, runtime path, migration, or export change is made. Phase 045 Character collision can still proceed independently on the qualified Kinematic movement service; this decision does not implement Phase 045.
