# One-way top crossing contract (Phase 046)

Character movement keeps Phase 045's solid X→Y path. It separately classifies eligible scene Box and Tile collision quads as one-way support planes. One-way shapes are excluded from X blocking and all ceiling/wall probes. The original Character shape(s) before this tick supply support bottoms: transformed Box minimum world Y or Circle center minus radius. The resolved platform supplies horizontal top Y and X footprint.

For requested downward delta `dy < 0`, a shape whose starting bottom is at/above `topY - 0.000101` may cross at `t = max(0, (bottomY - topY) / -dy)`. Eligibility requires `t ≤ 1` and horizontal overlap of that shape shifted by the accepted X delta times `t` with the platform footprint. The minimum fraction among shapes/platforms is used, with stable obstacle ID tie-break within `1e-9`. This analytic crossing prevents high-speed tunneling and checks diagonal motion at crossing, not just at frame end. The Y request is capped at that fraction before the unchanged solid Y solver; an earlier solid hit still wins. Authored velocity remains unchanged.

At zero Y motion, a read-only downward probe can report grounded if a shape bottom is between `topY - 0.000101` and `topY + probeDistance + 0.000101` and still overlaps the footprint. It never snaps a Character from arbitrary distance. Walking off clears contact. Starting below, direct teleport into platform volume, upward motion, and side motion do not trigger one-way depenetration or blocking. Support IDs remain transient move diagnostics via the existing sorted hit IDs; no persistent contact registry is created.
