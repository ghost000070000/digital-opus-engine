# Moving one-way platform contract

An eligible Moving Platform 2D may also own enabled One-Way Platform 2D. Its valid Box top is then a Character-only one-way surface: Characters pass upward/through sides, land while descending relative to the current support surface, and receive exact carry after support is established.

Platform movement is committed before Character intent. Previous support owns carry; current platform geometry owns the final contact state. Downward/upward platform translation carries an already-grounded rider. Unsupported platform pickup and side pushing are not promised. Rotated one-way tops use the Phase 047 walkable-surface and maximum-slope rules.
