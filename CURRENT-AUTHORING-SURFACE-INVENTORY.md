## Honest creator qualification

A beginner can now discover and complete a small 3D no-code game through the Guided First Game. A broader blank-project gauntlet proves Player, environment, Components, multiple Presets, interaction, state, HUD, win, Save/Load, and Playable Export. C-grade systems still require hidden naming, cross-panel coordination, or weak validation. Sprite, ordered Tilemap Layers, eligible Character collision, modern XY Platformer/Topdown controllers and named input have discoverable no-code paths. General Dynamic rigid-body physics, drop-through controls and pixel-perfect Camera projection remain unsupported. Existing read-only diagnostics, persistent starter discovery and the local Creator Guide complement those workflows. Current catalog grades, cited phase evidence and explicit limitations above are authoritative; subsystem grades alone do not establish integrated product readiness.
