# Character Step Handling contract — Phase 047

Character Collision 2D v2 offers Step Handling Enabled (new default on, migrated v1 default off) and Maximum Step Height (new default 0.5 world units; valid range greater than 0 and at most 10). A step attempt requires ground contact at the start, horizontal movement, no explicit upward intent, and a blocked solid horizontal path. Airborne Characters never snap to nearby floors solely because they are within step range.

For step-up, the candidate must be a flat solid top that overlaps the requested horizontal destination. Its authored height above the Character's current lowest point must not exceed Maximum Step Height. The solver checks the complete upward path for head clearance, the raised horizontal path against every eligible solid Collider (including multiple Colliders on one Character), and a supported destination. Only after all checks pass does it commit the combined move. A failed candidate changes no Transform; ordinary blocking remains. One-way sides are never step candidates. Contact tolerance is used for safe clearance, not to relax the authored maximum height.

For step-down, a Character grounded at the start may follow a nearby lower walkable solid support after horizontal movement when its vertical drop is no greater than Maximum Step Height and the descent sweep is clear. It does not snap over a deeper cliff or during upward intent. Runtime displacement is bounded by the actual contact. This is a local continuity rule, not gravity or a promise of general stair navigation in arbitrary geometry.
